Privacy policy.

What happens to your information when you visit this site. The short answer: very little.

The short version

  • No accounts, sign-ups, analytics, ads, or tracking cookies.
  • I don’t sell or share personal information.
  • Two small preferences (your light/dark choice and ARGU Daily progress) are saved in your own browser, and you can clear them anytime.
  • The companies that host the site and serve its fonts receive basic technical data, like your IP address, when your browser loads a page.

Who I am

This site is run by M Enam Ull Hassan, a doctoral student in Applied Linguistics based in Boston, Massachusetts. You can reach me about privacy at hello@enamhassan.com.

What I collect

I don’t collect personal information through this site. There are no forms, sign-ups, comment boxes, analytics tools, or advertising trackers.

If you email me

If you write to me, I receive your email address, your name if you include it, and your message. I use this only to read and reply to you, and I keep it only as long as the conversation needs. You can ask me to delete it.

Email sent to hello@enamhassan.com is forwarded by Cloudflare’s email routing service to my personal Gmail account, so Cloudflare and Google handle it on the way.

Saved in your browser

The site saves two small items in your browser’s local storage so it can remember your choices:

  • Theme: whether you picked light or dark mode.
  • ARGU Daily progress: which stages you’ve completed, your stars, and whether you’ve seen the help screen.

These stay on your device and are never sent to me. You can view and clear them on the Cookie Settings page.

What you type into the tools

Words, sentences, and maps you enter into the tools are processed entirely in your browser. They aren’t sent to me or stored on any server.

One small exception: when you download a semantic map drawn in the handwriting style, your browser fetches that font (Kalam) from Google Fonts. To keep the file small, the request lists the individual letters your map uses, but not your words or sentences.

Services that help run the site

  • GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.) hosts the site. GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.
  • Google Fonts (Google LLC) provides the site’s fonts. When your browser downloads them, Google receives technical information such as your IP address and browser type. See the Google Fonts FAQ and Google Privacy Policy.
  • Cloudflare manages the domain name and forwards email sent to hello@enamhassan.com. See the Cloudflare Privacy Policy.

When you follow a link to another site, such as LinkedIn, that site’s own privacy policy applies.

Children

Students of any age can use the learning tools. Because the site collects no personal information, it doesn’t knowingly collect any from children. If you believe a child has emailed me personal information, contact me and I’ll delete it.

Your choices and rights

You can clear the site’s saved preferences at any time on the Cookie Settings page or in your browser settings. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal information about you. Since the only personal information I might hold is email you’ve sent me, just write to hello@enamhassan.com and I’ll help.

Security

The site is served over an encrypted HTTPS connection.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, I’ll update it here and change the date at the top of the page.
