Statistics, step by step

Not sure which statistical test to use, or what the result means? Answer a few plain questions to find your test, then try it with example numbers or your own. Every result starts with a short answer in plain English, then walks you through the details. Tap any underlined term for a quick explanation.

1

Find your test

Pick the answers that fit your study. No statistics knowledge needed.

2

Work through a test

Choose a test, then use the example or switch to your own numbers. Everything is calculated in your browser; nothing is sent anywhere.

Calculations follow standard formulas and were checked against SciPy, a widely used statistics library. Effect size benchmarks come from Cohen (1988) and, for second language research, Plonsky and Oswald (2014, Language Learning). This tool is for learning; for a thesis or article, run your final analysis in software such as jamovi, JASP, R, or SPSS and check the assumptions there. Spotted an error?
