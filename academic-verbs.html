Grammar guide
+ that + clause

Many reporting verbs are followed by that and a full clause.

Smith argues that feedback improves revision.

+ noun

Research verbs usually take a direct object, with no preposition after them.

The study examines the data. not “examines about”

Present or past?

Use the present to report ideas, and the past to describe what a study did.

Nation argues… / The study found…
