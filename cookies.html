Cookies and local storage

Cookies are small files that websites save in your browser, often to track you across visits or across other sites. This site doesn’t set any cookies, and it doesn’t use any tracking or advertising technology.

It does use your browser’s local storage, a similar feature, to remember two choices you make. Nothing in local storage is sent to me or to anyone else.

What this site saves

NameWhat it doesWhen it’s savedHow long
themeRemembers whether you chose light or dark mode.When you click the light/dark button.Until you clear it.
argu-stages.v2Saves your ARGU Daily stars, completed stages, and whether you’ve seen the help screen.When you play ARGU Daily.Until you clear it.

Both are functional preferences you choose to set. Because the site uses nothing for tracking or advertising, it doesn’t show a cookie banner.

Other companies

The site is hosted by GitHub Pages and its fonts come from Google Fonts. Neither sets cookies through this site, but both receive basic technical information when your browser loads a page. The Privacy Policy explains this in more detail.

Your choices

You can see and clear everything this site has saved on the Cookie Settings page. You can also clear site data in your browser’s settings, or use a private window so nothing is kept after you close it. Clearing these items only resets your theme and ARGU Daily progress. Everything else works the same.

Changes and contact

If the site ever starts using cookies, I’ll update this page first. Questions? Email hello@enamhassan.com.
