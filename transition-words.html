Punctuation guide
Sentence starters

Begin a new sentence, or follow a semicolon. Most take a comma after them.

The data were limited. However, the trend was clear.

Joining words

Link two ideas in one sentence. If the sentence starts with one, put a comma after the first part.

Although the data were limited, the trend was clear.

Before a noun or -ing

Follow with a noun phrase or an -ing form, not a full sentence.

Despite the limited data, the trend was clear.
