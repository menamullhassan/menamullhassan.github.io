Contronyms: one word, opposite meanings

A contronym (also called a Janus word or auto-antonym) is a word that can mean the opposite of itself. You can dust a shelf or dust a cake; a team can be sanctioned for good or for bad. Each entry below shows both meanings with an example sentence, so you can see how context decides which one is meant.
