DSP Critical Analysis

Instructor · Summer 2026 · Six-week course

A course preparing students for the reading, writing, reasoning, and discussion expectations of first-year college work. Modeled on the learning outcomes of CRW 112 and ENGL 101, it builds the foundational academic habits students need: careful reading, evidence-based interpretation, inquiry, discussion, drafting, feedback, revision, and reflection.

By the end of the course, students can

  1. Read short texts closely, identifying claims, evidence, assumptions, and implications.
  2. Develop a focused argumentative claim in response to a text, issue, or question.
  3. Support an argument with relevant evidence from readings or limited research.
  4. Explain and respond to opposing views through counterargument and rebuttal.
  5. Organize an essay with a clear introduction, body, transitions, and conclusion.
  6. Use feedback to revise for clarity, evidence, organization, and reasoning.
  7. Reflect on their growth as readers, writers, and college learners.
