Plain-language summaries of 92 theories, hypotheses, and models in second language acquisition and applied linguistics. Each entry explains who proposed it and when, what it claims, its key ideas, and the main criticisms. Search for a theory, filter by approach, or browse from A to Z or by year.

The theories on this page are drawn from the three books below, which are also good places to read further. Each date marks when an idea was first proposed or became widely known, so some entries list more than one. If you spot an error, please let me know through the contact page.

  • Ortega, L. (2014). Understanding second language acquisition. Routledge.
  • VanPatten, B., Keating, G. D., & Wulff, S. (Eds.). (2020). Theories in second language acquisition: An introduction (3rd ed.). Routledge.
