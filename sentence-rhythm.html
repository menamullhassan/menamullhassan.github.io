Sentence Rhythm

Paste your writing to see every sentence as a bar sized by its length. Spot long stretches, choppy runs, and places where the rhythm could change.

Open a .txt or Word (.docx) file, or drop it on the box. Dots in abbreviations (e.g., et al., U.S.), initials, decimals like p < .05, web addresses, and numbered lists are not counted as sentence ends.

Length groups

    Academic writing often runs longer than everyday prose. Set the point where a sentence starts to feel long for your readers.

    Paste some text to see its rhythm.
